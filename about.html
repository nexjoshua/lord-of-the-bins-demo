Our story

Built to change an industry with a bad name

Lord of the Bins was created when two separate waste clearance companies came together with one aim: to make a real difference in an industry known for cowboys and fly-tipping.

Over 10 years later, we are a fully licensed and insured team trusted by homeowners, builders and more than 500 local businesses across Sussex.

Sustainability is at the heart of everything we do. We have set processes to make sure everything possible is recycled, so 97% of the waste we collect is diverted from landfill and 100% of reusable items get a second life.

Giving back

Litter picks, fayres and fly-tips

We do not just clear waste for paying customers. In our spare time we clear fly-tipped rubbish for local residents at our own cost, run community litter picks along the Sussex coast, and turn up to support local fayres, events and charities.

It is our way of looking after the places our customers live and work.
